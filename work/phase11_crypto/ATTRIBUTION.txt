Phase11 calls the frozen phase10 baseline binary directly. Its variant imports
the byte-identical prefix of the frozen phase10 source, excluding only its CLI
main declaration and following bytes. The imported classes include the frozen
phase9 metering and audited phase4 source movements. No prior source is edited.

The new variant limits every source climb to 5000 proposal evaluations and
refreshes parents after 4 processed offspring, including partial climbs. Local
caps, completed sweeps and global cuts are logged separately. The objective,
movements, population admission, parent choice and perturbations stay frozen.
This pilot does not reproduce the whole published Lasry attack or claim all
CrypTool parameters. It contains no historical BLUME or K1 search.
