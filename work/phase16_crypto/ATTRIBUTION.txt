Phase16 reads frozen source_moves.h/ict_search.h/crypt primitives as dependencies.
It copies byte-for-byte three exact-integer sections of phase15/static_landscape.cpp
into exact_scorer.h. build_record identifies source and fragment hashes. Only
that exact scorer is called, with an adapter retaining its charge_backend(false)
entry. No legacy scorer is executed. The new Core implements two policy rules
without target recognition. Source-order A keeps immediate acceptance but removes
the source HC shuffle; it is not a reproduction of the full source algorithm.

generate_keys.cpp and moves.json are exact local copies of phase15 own external
helper/geometry. Older sources/files are never modified. Literary models and
holdouts keep their previous Gutenberg provenance and separate terms. The pilot
does not reproduce the full Lasry attack or attack the historical BLUME telegrams.
